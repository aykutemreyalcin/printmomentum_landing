Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

PrintMomentum ("we", "us") operates an independent research index for printable t-shirt listings discovered on Etsy. This policy explains what we collect on this website and in the app.

What we collect

  • App accounts: email address and authentication data for invited users.
  • Beta requests: if you submit the access form, your message is sent via your own email client to us.
  • Listing index data: public Etsy listing metadata needed to operate the index (title, image URL, price, shop name, public engagement fields).
  • Technical logs: standard server logs for security and reliability.

What we do not collect

  • Etsy seller credentials from visitors
  • Buyer emails, payment details, or order history from Etsy
  • Ability to modify Etsy listings on your behalf

How we use data

We use collected information to operate the index, authenticate invited users, improve ranking and filters, respond to support or beta requests, and maintain security.

Etsy relationship

PrintMomentum is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Etsy, Inc. We use Etsy's official API under applicable developer terms and remove or refresh listing data as required.

Retention

Listing metadata is refreshed on a scheduled ingest cadence and retained only as long as needed to operate the service and comply with Etsy requirements.
