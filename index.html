Questions

What is PrintMomentum?

A research tool for people who sell t-shirts on Etsy. It shows which designs are rising fastest in Etsy search right now, so you can see what buyers are starting to like.

How do you measure how fast a design is rising?

We look at how quickly a design moves up in Etsy search and how fast it gains new favorites and reviews. Recent change counts more than total size, so a new design that is taking off can rank above an old bestseller. We show the result in words: Very fast, Fast or Normal.

Are the sales numbers exact?

No. Etsy does not publish sales per design, so we estimate them from public signals and show a range, such as ~80–120 a month. Use it to compare designs, not as an exact figure.

How often is the data updated?

6 times a day.

What are trademark warnings?

If a design title or tag contains a well-known brand, team or character name, we mark it. Selling such designs can get your listing removed or your shop in trouble. The warning is a helper, not legal advice, so always check before you publish.
