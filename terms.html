What PrintMomentum is

PrintMomentum is a research tool for Etsy t-shirt sellers. It shows which designs are rising fastest in Etsy search, with estimated sales, trademark warnings and niche ideas. If you connect your own Etsy shop, it also checks your listings for trademark risks and shows your sales. We do not sell products and we are not a marketplace.

Your account

  • One account is for one person. Do not share your sign-in with others.
  • You are responsible for what happens under your account. Tell us quickly if you think someone else is using it.

Price and payment

  • PrintMomentum Pro costs $29 per month, tax included.
  • You pay in advance for each month. The subscription renews every month until you cancel.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your card number.
  • If we change the price, we will email you at least 30 days before. The new price applies from your next renewal after that, and you can cancel before it does.

Free trial

  • New customers get a 7-day free trial. You add a card to start it.
  • If you cancel before the 7 days are over, you are not charged. Cancelling during the trial costs nothing.
  • If you do not cancel, your card is charged $29 when the trial ends, and then every month.
  • One free trial per person.

Cancelling

  • You can cancel any time from your profile in the app.
  • When you cancel, your subscription stops renewing. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for, and you are not charged again.
  • Until that date you can change your mind and keep your subscription, from the same place.
  • There are no partial refunds for the rest of a period you already paid for, unless the law where you live says we must.
  • If you cancel during the free trial, it costs nothing.

Pausing

  • Need a break? On a monthly plan you can pause for 1 or 2 months instead of cancelling, from your profile.
  • You keep access until the end of the month you already paid for. During the pause you are not charged and the app is closed for you; your saved products and store connection stay.
  • When the pause ends, payments and access start again automatically. If you come back early, a new month is charged that day.

Failed payments

If a payment fails, Stripe will try again for a few days and we will email you. If it still fails, your access is paused until you update your card.

Estimates and warnings

Rising speed, estimated sales and other numbers are our own estimates from public data. They can be incomplete, late or wrong. Trademark warnings are a helper, not legal advice: a design without a warning can still infringe someone's rights. Check before you publish, and do not copy other sellers' designs.
