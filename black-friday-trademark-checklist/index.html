Free checklist

Black Friday trademark checklist

25 names that sell well on holiday t-shirts and are registered trademarks for clothing in the US. Check your shop before the busiest weekend of the year, when a takedown costs the most.

5 checks before Black Friday

  1. Search your titles and tags for every name below, not only your best sellers.
  2. Check each word on its own. Short forms count: “Skellington” still points to Jack Skellington.
  3. Made-up words count too: “Grinchmas” still contains Grinch.
  4. Look at the design, not only the text. A character drawing with no name can still be a problem.
  5. Re-check viral phrases every season. A pending application can become a registration within months.

Each number links to the official USPTO record. All checked on 11 November 2026: live registrations that cover clothing (class 25), except Girlypop, a pending application. A name missing from this list can still be protected. Not legal advice.
